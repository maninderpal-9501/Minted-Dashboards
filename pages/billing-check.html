DoorDash POs against the accounting PO tracker

Building the month's rows and checking each one against the tracker…
How the check works:
  • One row per DoorDash PO issued in the month, from the SPS order export, CTL's shipments, FedEx and the accounting tracker.
  • A row matches when PO $, total quantity, expected ship date, shipping charge and FedEx tracking all agree with the line accounting entered in the BD Wholesale & Accounting PO tracker.
  • Shipping charge follows Minted's rule: up to 50 cards $8.60, 51–150 $9.00, 151+ $10.00. Cancelled orders (including a seasonal card in an everyday order) are written as accounting writes them: $0 and "Cancelled".
