The queries behind this page

Source files: DoorDash's SPS Commerce orders, Minted's BD tracker (Jan–Sep), CTL Global's weekly shipment and stock reports, FedEx exports, Minted's PO templates and the DoorDash Everyday inventory sheet. The graph stores what those files say; the Minted tools compute the rest (mixed-order flags, reorder quantities, billing rows).
